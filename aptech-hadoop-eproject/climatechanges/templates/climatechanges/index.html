How EarthScape works

From raw readings to real decisions

Five steps, running on a distributed big-data pipeline.

  1. 01
    🛰️

    Ingest

    Satellite, weather-station and sensor data in CSV and other climate formats, historical and live.

  2. 02
    🗄️

    Store on HDFS

    Hadoop's distributed file system keeps every dataset safe, replicated and ready to scale.

  3. 03
    ⚙️

    MapReduce

    Parallel jobs crunch decades of records, skipping gaps and broken rows gracefully.

  4. 04
    🧠

    Machine learning

    Regression forecasts the trend; z-scores flag anomalies; correlation shows what moves together.

  5. 05
    🔔

    Alert & act

    Thresholds trigger alerts so analysts and administrators can respond fast.
