How fast is the planet warming?

Interactive analysis of NASA GISTEMP surface temperature and NOAA Mauna Loa CO2: trends, anomaly detection, a 2050 projection and live threshold alerts.

Warming stripes

One stripe per year, blue (cooler than 1951-1980) to red (warmer). Hover for the year.

Global temperature anomaly

Annual means by hemisphere with a 5-year centred average. Red markers are statistical anomalies (more than 2σ above the 1951-1980 baseline).

Atmospheric CO2 (Keeling curve)

Monthly mean at Mauna Loa Observatory, ppm. The saw-tooth is the Northern Hemisphere growing season.

Projection to 2050
