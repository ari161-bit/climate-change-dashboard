Climate dashboard

How fast is the planet warming?

NASA temperature, NOAA CO2, national emissions, sea level, glaciers, disasters and 12 city records, processed from a partitioned data lake with MapReduce-style jobs, trend models and threshold alerts.

Warming stripes

One stripe per year, from cooler (blue) to warmer (red) than the 1951-1980 average. Hover for the year.

1 · Temperature and CO2

The warming signal

Global temperature anomaly

Annual means by hemisphere with a 5-year average. Diamonds are statistical anomalies (more than 2σ above the 1951-1980 baseline).

Atmospheric CO2 (Keeling curve)

Monthly mean at Mauna Loa Observatory, ppm. The saw-tooth is the Northern Hemisphere growing season.

Projection to 2050

What is already changing

Millimetres relative to the series baseline (tide gauges and satellites).

Cumulative change in metres of water equivalent; falling means net ice loss.

4 · Regions and cities

Warming by latitude band

City warming ranking

Trend of annual mean temperature since 1981 (NASA POWER reanalysis, 12 cities). Local effects such as aerosols and irrigation can mask warming in a grid cell.

5 · Relationships

Correlation across datasets

Pearson correlation of annual series on overlapping years. Strong values mostly reflect a shared long-term trend; correlation alone does not prove one series drives another.
