Trend analyzer

Climate and carbon trends by country or region

Pick a dataset, a country or IMF country group and an indicator. A trend model is fitted to the observed years, projected forward, and checked for outliers. The model is chosen by backtest unless you force one.

Observed values and projection

Model quality

R² and RMSE are measured on the observed years; the backtest scores each model on years it was not trained on.

Outliers

Years whose value departs from the trend by more than two standard deviations of the residuals.
